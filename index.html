MedQuery AI — Clinical Medical RAG Engine with Self-RAG & RAGAS

SAFETY ROADMAP ACTIVE
// PROBLEM STATEMENT
Standard naive RAG pipelines over complex clinical literature frequently retrieve irrelevant or out-of-context chunks, causing hallucinated clinical recommendations where precision is life-critical.
// ARCHITECTURE & RAG SAFETY ROADMAP
Currently expanding the production ChromaDB + Groq engine into a multi-layered clinical safety & precision architecture:
  • Cross-Encoder Reranking: BGE-Reranker re-scoring top-50 vector matches to surface the true top-5 context chunks.

  • Guardrails & RAGAS Framework: Continuous evaluation of Faithfulness, Answer Relevance & Context Precision with strict medical disclaimers and PII filters.
